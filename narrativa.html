These are the last words the PLAYER heard from MOTHER: MOTHER Listen to me - do not come out now, it’s dangerous outside. You have to stay here until you are ready. Don’t look at me like that, please. I will be okay, I promise. Once you are ready to come out, you will know. Trust me. Here, take this. It will protect you from all evil. Now go, I will be waiting here for you. Stay safe. The words fade away to leave an empty black screen. The thud from a door closed shut is heard, followed by the echo of slow footsteps walking away. After two to three seconds the flickering lights turn on and the PLAYER is found in the DETOX ROOM of the bunker, facing the door that leads to the outside.

Game Lore

THE STORY OF OUR TOWN Long ago, our town was protected by a mountain giant. This mountain giant was a large man and a great hunter who had brought a group of humans from the plains who were haunted by evil spirits. He was rarely seen by folk, as he was conscious of his ferocious and wild appearance, so he guarded the town from the shadows and only walked through the streets at night. In the old ways, he was known as The Guardian of the Breath, for he protected the town from the unseen dangers and evil spirits that lurked just beyond the edge of sight. Generations passed, and history was lost – one day, a group of drunk young men roamed the streets and laughed out loud. The giant saw them about to stray into the woods, so he approached them to guide them back into the town. But at the sight of the giant the men screamed in fear, they grabbed some stones from the floor and started throwing them at their protector. The giant was angry and realized the town folk had lost the respect they once professed to him, so he turned around and walked into the woods to never come back again. THE MOUNTAIN GIANT’S LEGACY Although the great protector of our town was gone, remnants of his bloodline remained, hidden among us - descendants who had inherited the ability to see the spirits that inhabit our world. When the giant left, his descendants watched as the spirits started to walk among the people. But they meant no harm, and as long as they were not disturbed, humans and spirits would be able to live in peace. However, there was something unknown even to the giant’s bloodline. Unlike the peaceful spirits, some humans, driven by dark emotions, would sometimes transform into something more monstrous. When bad feelings grew too strong and could not be purged, they began to twist. Men with darkness within their hearts would slowly start to grow big twisting horns on their heads and their limps would shrink into their torsos. Their legs would become a single long tail, as their skin got covered by green scales. The descendants of the giant were the only ones who could see the spirits for what they truly were - neither friend nor foe, but a natural part of the world’s balance. And they alone could sense the dark-hearted men, those whose emotions had become corrupted and who had transformed into monstrous forms. However, these new guardians knew that revealing their abilities would be dangerous. If the townsfolk ever discovered the truth, they might even believe they were the ones bringing the spirits with them, disturbing the peace. Worse, history could repeat itself – if the people ever learned who they truly were, the fear and distrust that led to the giant’s departure could return. The guardians did not want to scare the town into rejecting them or causing the very chaos they had worked so hard to prevent. So, they remained hidden, just as their ancestor had done in the past. To keep the balance that had remained for generations, they accepted and devoted themselves to this quiet duty. INVISIBLE PEOPLE *this one is skippable* Sometimes you may hear a voice speaking softly behind your ear. A voice that comes and goes as a gust of wind. A voice that acknowledges your worries and finds you when you are lost. A voice that wants to guide you to find that which you are looking for. This someone will tell you when you must go straight and when you must take a turn. That someone is what we call a “Veiled Voice”. Some believe that Veiled Voices are entities similar to humans, that surround themselves with cloaks that make them invisible to common folk. Others have reasoned that they are all spirit and no flesh, and therefore intangible and imperceptible to the human eye. But no matter what they are made of, everyone knows that these Voices are kind and pure-hearted beings. ON MONSTER-LIKE PEOPLE The greatest fear of a guardian is watching their town destroy itself. Folk, once kind, slowly transform into dark creatures, feeding on the very emotions that once bound them together. Each night, in the silence, the creatures creep into the homes of the vulnerable – those whose hearts are heavy with doubt, sorrow, or rage – and whisper nightmares into their minds. They twist their thoughts, draining away their joy and opening a path for misery to take root. They are patient. They wait for the town to reach its breaking point, where hope will fade into the past, and only darkness will remain. Then, they will reveal themselves – emerging from the shadows, their horns and scales gleaming in the cold moonlight. The spirits that have lingered nearby will rise with them, and together, they will reduce the town to nothing but ruin, erasing all that it once was. ON THE LEGENDARY WOLF On one fateful night, long before the giant left the town, a great storm descended upon the town, and the spirits within it threatened to swallow it whole. The town’s guardian predicted that his power alone would not be enough, so he kneeled, facing the skies, and recited a chant to call upon nature’s help. At that moment, emerging from the very depths of the forest, a great beast arrived next to the giant. It was the figure of a wolf, its fur shimmering with the light of the full moon. It charged into the storm, standing between the people and the spirits that sought to destroy them. The storm raged for days, but the wolf did not falter. Finally, as the storm began to subside, the animal collapsed to the ground. Its body laid still, but the giant could feel its spirit remaining. So, he allowed it to live on within a pendant made of one of its claws. THE ORIGIN AND THE GUARDIAN EAGLE At the very first start of time, the world we live in was completely covered by water. The sea stretched far beyond, becoming an untouched realm where no creature could live in. The world waited, still and silent, without a purpose. But in the heart of the skies, where now the clouds melt as they touch the sun rays, there emerged a small feathered creature. It was the first of its kind, a newborn chick. As it swayed in the winds, it could hear the pulse of the earth beneath the water, longing to take shape on the surface. The small chick let itself fall, diving to the bottom of the sea, and brought to the surface a clump of mud with its beak. The mud did not sink back, but instead, it began spreading until a small island was formed. And thus, the heart of the world we live in was born. The chick sank into the waters once more, and after some time, it emerged again, transformed into a new being. Its spread wings could cover the light of our sun, its call could be heard from every inch of the land, and its reddish feathers shimmered with golden tips. It is now known that all eagles come from this creature, though its size was far greater than any bird known to man. Above the isle, the Great Eagle flew and became the guardian of creation as it watched the land grow. With its sacred vision, the eagle could see the threads that connected the island to the sky – four mighty cords that held the world in place, one for each of the four corners of the earth. These cords were the binding forces of creation, and as long as they remained strong, the world would endure. But as the island began to grow, dark spirits started to roam the earth, drawn to the emerging life. These spirits sought to bring destruction to the fragile world, to undo the work of creation and drown the island beneath the waves once more. The evil spirits have always watched, waiting for the moment when the island might be vulnerable. And so the eagle has continued its eternal guard, circling high above the sacred isle – waiting for the moment the threat comes, ready to fight for life.
